Page title: Menu: A Guide to Skyline Chili
Page description: What a 3-way, 4-way, 5-way, and cheese coney are at Skyline Chili, plus how to order like a Cincinnati local.

The Menu

At Skyline, you order chili by counting. Each "way" adds one more topping to the plate.

Jump to: 3-Way, 4-Way, 5-Way, or Coneys.

How to Order Like a Local

Say the number, not the ingredients. Ask for "a 4-way bean" or "a 4-way onion," and say "two coneys" instead of "two hot dogs."

3-Way

[Image: images/three-way.webp. Caption: A 3-way: spaghetti, chili, and cheese.]

The classic. Spaghetti is topped with Skyline's chili, then a mound of finely shredded cheddar cheese.

4-Way

[Image: images/four-way.webp. Caption: A 4-way, with oyster crackers on top.]

A 3-way plus one more layer: either diced onions or red beans. Order a "4-way onion" or a "4-way bean."

5-Way

Everything: spaghetti, chili, onions, beans & cheese.

Coneys

[Image: images/cheese-coneys.webp. Caption: Three cheese coneys.]

A cheese coney is a small hot dog in a steamed bun with mustard, chili, diced onions, and shredded cheddar. Most people order two or three.

Oyster Crackers

[Image: images/oyster-crackers.webp. Caption: Dine in, and a bowl of oyster crackers comes with your meal.]

Eat in, and your meal comes with a bowl of oyster crackers. Some people crumble them on top of their chili, and some eat them by the handful while they wait.

How to Eat a Way

Locals don't twirl their spaghetti. Here's how they eat a 3-way, in order:

Shake a little hot sauce over the cheese.
Crumble a few oyster crackers on top.
Cut straight down through the layers with the side of your fork.
Scoop up a bite with spaghetti, chili, and cheese all at once.

More on the Menu

The ways and coneys are the stars, but Skyline serves more than that. Other favorites include:

Chili cheese fries
A baked potato topped with chili & cheese
A chili burrito
A Greek salad
