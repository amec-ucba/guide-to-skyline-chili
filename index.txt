A Guide to Skyline Chili

Skyline Chili has been serving Cincinnati chili since 1949. It isn't the thick, spicy chili most people picture. It's a thin meat sauce with a hint of sweet spice, served over spaghetti or a hot dog and buried under a mound of shredded cheddar.

The Clifton Parlor

[Image: images/clifton-exterior.webp. Caption: The Clifton parlor on Ludlow Avenue, a short walk from UC's campus.]

The Clifton parlor sits in the Gaslight District on Ludlow Avenue, a short walk from UC's campus. It's a favorite for a quick lunch between classes & a late dinner after a game.

Crosstown Shootout

Skyline sponsors the Crosstown Shootout, the yearly basketball game between UC and Xavier, Cincinnati's two Division I men's teams.

Your First Visit

Never been to Skyline? A few tips before you go:

Start with a 3-way or two cheese coneys.
Grab extra napkins & don't wear a white shirt.
Try a few oyster crackers on top of your chili.
Save room for one more coney. You'll want it.
